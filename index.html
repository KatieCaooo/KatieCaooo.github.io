About me

From writing code to shaping products

Case studies

How I work, in three stories

Each story shows the problem, what I did as the product person, and what changed.

01

Making digital codes safe with real-time vendor APIs

Tiki · Value-Added Services (VAS) · Associate Product Manager

The problem

Digital codes (top-ups, game cards, vouchers) were stored in our own database. That meant risk of leaked codes, stock that didn't match, and codes that expired before they were sold.

What I did

  • Owned the VAS product area and its roadmap
  • Integrated 3 vendor APIs covering 100+ SKUs: workflows, business rules, error handling and fallbacks
  • Ran API testing and UAT, checking data with SQL
  • Specified about 20 screens for retail buyers, wholesale buyers and admins

The outcome

  • Codes now come live from vendors, cutting leaks, mismatches and expiry
  • Same approach reused for Vouchers, widening the product range
  • Launched a wholesale channel and secured better vendor commissions

02

Taking over a platform with no documentation

Tiki · TicketBox (acquired event-ticketing platform)

The problem

Tiki acquired TicketBox, but the platform came without any documentation. Nobody had a full picture of how it worked, and some workflows were broken or incomplete.

What I did

  • Mapped and documented the system screen by screen
  • Found broken and incomplete workflows and proposed fixes
  • Coordinated the VNPay payment integration with the dev team

The outcome

  • The team finally had a shared, written map of the product
  • More successful payments and a faster checkout

03

Designing a two-sided B2B marketplace

Healiverse · Healthy Beauty (dietary supplements) · Technical Product Owner

The problem

A new marketplace needed to serve two very different users: sellers managing products, stock, orders and invoices, and buyers who want advice before they order.

What I did

  • Researched competing online pharmacy platforms
  • Wrote the proposal, plan and user journeys for both sides
  • Specified 20+ screens and modules and an AI chatbot with 4 functions
  • Led a 4-person cross-functional team and presented progress to partners

The outcome

  • Clear specs the team could build from, with acceptance criteria for each feature
  • Delivered 2 third-party integrations: e-invoices and shipping orders
